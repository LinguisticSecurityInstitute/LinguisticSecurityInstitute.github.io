Linguistic Security Institute

The Linguistic Security Institute (LSI) is an open-source research initiative dedicated to ensuring AI systems are culturally coherent, institutionally resilient, and ethically verifiable. We audit linguistic bias in AI, develop open-source tools for bias detection, create educational resources for developers and students, and propose governance frameworks for linguistic safety in AI development.
